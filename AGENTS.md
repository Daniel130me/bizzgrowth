# Instructions for AI agents working on Nellovia

## Read first: the North Star

Every change must serve **the feeling we want owners to have**. The full text is in
`PRODUCT.md`, under "North Star: the feeling we want owners to have". Read it before you build or
change anything. In short:

1. **Fear of missing valuable discoveries.** Owners expect something new and useful each visit.
2. **Confidence Nellovia will find answers.** It investigates beyond the obvious, finds overlooked
   problems and opportunities, challenges wrong assumptions, recommends evidence-based solutions,
   and follows through.
3. **A living business intelligence.** It is a consultant that keeps thinking, not a tool that waits.
4. **Every visit has potential value.** Something new, a risk, an opportunity, progress, a market
   development, or a recommendation.
5. **It remembers**: owners never start over. 6. **Every discovery can be checked**: own figures
   only, maths in code. 7. **It cares about the person**: the urgent thing comes first. 8. **Progress
   is visible.**

**Earned, never manufactured.** No fabricated discoveries, meaningless notifications, manufactured
urgency, or generic advice dressed up as insight. When nothing new has happened, say so.

Before finishing any change, ask: *after this, what does the owner know that they didn't, or what
can they now do that they couldn't?* If a screen or flow misleads or underwhelms, fix it.
