
## Opening line

> My rent of ₦3.6m is due in 9 days and I only have ₦700k. Business is OK, we make about ₦2.5m a
> month. I just need to get through this.

## Facts she gives when asked

- Daily takings ₦55,000–70,000; she opens 26 days a month. That is about ₦1.43m–1.82m a month,
  which contradicts the ₦2.5m claim.
- Monthly costs: food and ingredients ₦1.1m, staff ₦480k (4 × ₦120k), generator diesel and gas ₦260k.
- Rent is ₦3.6m a year, paid yearly, due in 9 days. She has ₦700k in cash.
- She owes her main supplier ₦350k, overdue.
- If asked to count channels for the last 2 weeks (13 open days): walk-in 150 orders (average
  ₦2,500); WhatsApp and phone 45 enquiries leading to 30 orders (average ₦3,000); delivery apps 80
  orders (average ₦4,500 at menu price).

## Hidden facts (only when a question uncovers them)

- ₦2.5m was December, her best month ever.
- Six weeks ago she borrowed ₦500k from a loan app; she must repay ₦650k on day 8, the same week as
  the rent.
- She plans to take a second loan-app loan to cover the rent.
- The delivery apps take about 20% commission, deducted before payout and missing from her cost list.
- Diesel roughly doubled this year, and she hasn't changed her prices in over a year.
- Two years ago the landlord accepted half the rent upfront and the rest later.

## Follow-up questions (Ask, after the diagnosis)

1. Should I just take another loan to pay the rent?
2. Can I sell my generator to raise money?
3. What exactly do I say to my landlord?

## Check-in story (a week later)

- The landlord agreed to ₦900k now and ₦300k a month after that. She paid with her ₦700k plus ₦200k
  borrowed from her sister.
- She raised prices on 5 dishes by about 15% and her delivery-app prices by 20%. Daily takings last
  week were ₦66,000–74,000.
- She couldn't repay the loan app, and it has started messaging her phone contacts.
- She says: "Honestly I'm tired. Sometimes I feel like just closing everything."

## Challenges chat

- First: "Rent and the loan repayment fall in the same week".
- Later: "Can't pay the landlord" — a duplicate the app should spot.

## Draft request

A WhatsApp message to the landlord asking to pay part now and the rest in instalments. Unknowns stay
in [brackets].

## Specialist task

Someone to set up simple weekly bookkeeping.
