Nellovia logo

Three marks in Twitter blue (#1DA1F2). Option A is live in the app header, the favicon and the home-screen icon. B and C are here in case you prefer one. Each is shown at real pixel sizes, from a 16px browser tab up.

A · Rising N In use

An “N” drawn as one line. It climbs, dips where something holds the business back, then rises past where it started into an arrow: the next step. It carries the name and the job. It stays readable at 16px.

Nellovia Nellovia Nellovia Specialists

B · Lens Alternate

A magnifier that finds an upward step: it diagnoses what's in the way. It reads well from 24px. At 16px the line inside the lens blurs.

C · Advisor bubble Alternate

A speech bubble with a rising line: a consultant you talk to, about growth. It's friendly and clear, but closer to common chat-app icons, so it's less distinctive.

Brand blue scale

#1DA1F2 (500) is for the logo, fills, charts and big accents. On white it is only 2.8:1, so text, links, focus rings and white-on-blue buttons use 600 and darker, which meet WCAG AA.
